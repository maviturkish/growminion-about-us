Our Story

Why We Chose to Focus Only on Law Firms

GrowMinion started with a simple observation: good lawyers were losing clients to firms that were easier to find online.

When someone is injured, facing a charge, going through a divorce or trying to stay in Canada, they don't flip through a directory. They search on Google, check the map, read reviews and, more and more, ask an AI assistant who to call. The firm that shows up first, and looks trustworthy when it does, usually gets the call.

Most marketing agencies treat law firms like any other business. They report on traffic and impressions, write generic content and ignore the rules lawyers must follow when they advertise. Lawyers end up paying for activity, not for new cases.

So we made a decision early: one industry, one outcome. We work with law firms, we lead with SEO, and we measure our work by the qualified consultations it brings in.

As our clients grew, so did what they needed. Today we pair SEO with Google Ads management for faster lead flow and CRM & AI automation so every new enquiry is answered, followed up and turned into a booked consultation.

Frequently Asked Questions About GrowMinion

About GrowMinion

What is GrowMinion?
GrowMinion (Grow Minion LLC) is a Canada-based law firm SEO agency helping lawyers attract more qualified consultations through Google Search, Google Maps, and AI search, backed by Google Ads management and CRM & AI automation.

Do you only work with law firms?
Yes. We work only with law firms, so every strategy, campaign and system we build, from SEO to Google Ads to automation, is designed for legal clients and the people searching for them.

Where is GrowMinion based?
Our home office is in Toronto, Canada. The company is registered in Wyoming, United States, and our operations team works from Dhaka, Bangladesh, which lets us deliver work quickly and consistently.

Who will work on my firm's account?
Your campaign is led by our founders' strategy team and handled by specialists in SEO, local search and legal content, with one point of contact who keeps you updated.
Who We Serve

Which practice areas do you work with?
We work with personal injury, family, immigration, criminal defence, real estate, employment, wills and estates and other practice areas where clients search online before hiring a lawyer.

Do you only work with law firms in Canada?
No. We're based in Canada and it's our home market, but our services aren't limited to Canadian firms. Wherever your firm is located, we can help you rank in your own cities and service areas.

Do you work with solo lawyers and small firms?
Yes. We work with solo practitioners, small firms and growing multi-office practices. The plan is shaped around your size, goals and local competition.

Will you work with competing firms in my city?
We'll discuss this openly on your strategy call. We keep client work confidential and avoid conflicts that would put two firms in the same practice area and market against each other.
Our Services

What services does GrowMinion offer?
SEO is our core service: law firm SEO, local SEO and Google Business Profile optimization, and AI search optimization. We also offer Google Ads management and CRM & AI automation, supported by legal content writing, link building, website improvements, and call and form tracking.

Do you manage Google Ads for law firms?
Yes. We run Google Search and Local Services Ads campaigns targeted by practice area and city, and track cost per consultation so you know what your budget is bringing in. Ads deliver leads quickly while SEO builds long-term visibility.

What is CRM & AI automation for law firms?
It's the system that handles leads after they come in: a CRM to track every enquiry, instant text and email follow-up, and AI-assisted intake and appointment booking, so fewer potential clients are lost before they speak to you.

What is local SEO for lawyers?
Local SEO helps your firm appear in Google Maps and the local "map pack" when people search for a lawyer in your city. It covers your Google Business Profile, reviews, local citations and city-specific pages.

What is AI search optimization for law firms?
It's the work of making your firm visible when people ask tools like ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI Overviews to recommend a lawyer, through clear content, strong reputation signals and consistent brand mentions.

Do you follow law society advertising rules?
Yes. We write and review content and ads with the advertising rules of your law society or bar in mind, and you approve everything before it goes live.
Results & Getting Started

How long does law firm SEO take to show results?
Many firms see early movement in Google Maps and rankings within the first few months. Consultation growth usually builds over six to twelve months, depending on your market and competition.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?
No honest agency can guarantee rankings, because Google and AI tools decide what to show. We commit to a clear plan, consistent work and transparent reporting on the results that matter.

How do you measure success?
We measure phone calls, form submissions and booked consultations, alongside rankings and map visibility, all shared in clear monthly reports.
