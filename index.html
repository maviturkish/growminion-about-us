Our Story

Why We Focus Only on Law Firm SEO

We started GrowMinion after seeing the same problem again and again: talented lawyers losing potential clients to firms that simply showed up first on Google. Generic marketing agencies treated law firms like any other business, chasing traffic instead of real cases.

So we chose one industry and went deep. Today, everything we build, from local SEO to content to AI search visibility, is designed around one outcome: more qualified consultations for your firm.

01

Built for Canadian Lawyers, Not Every Industry

We understand practice areas, legal advertising rules set by Canadian law societies, and how people in Toronto, Vancouver, Calgary and beyond search for a lawyer when they need one.

02

From Local Search to AI Search: How Clients Find Lawyers Today

Clients no longer just type "lawyer near me." They ask Google Maps, read AI Overviews, and ask ChatGPT for recommendations. We make sure your firm is the answer in every one of those places.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions About Our Law Firm SEO Services

Do you only work with law firms?
Yes. GrowMinion works exclusively with law firms, so every strategy, process and piece of content is built specifically for lawyers and their potential clients.

Do you work with lawyers outside Toronto?
Absolutely. We help law firms across Canada, including Ontario, British Columbia, Alberta and beyond, rank in their own cities and service areas.

How long does law firm SEO take to show results?
Most firms start seeing movement in Google Maps and rankings within the first few months, with consultation growth building steadily over six to twelve months depending on competition.

What is AI search optimization for law firms?
It's the work of making your firm visible when people ask AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI Overviews to recommend a lawyer, through clear content, strong reputation signals and consistent brand mentions.

How do you measure success?
We measure what matters to your firm: phone calls, form submissions and booked consultations, alongside rankings and map visibility, all shared in clear monthly reports.
